# Kestrel user guide

Start with the task you need to do. Each page guide stands on its own and uses screenshots of invented data. Kestrel keeps your search on your computer by default and never applies or contacts anyone for you.

## Set up

1. [First run and onboarding](guide/onboarding.md): choose how scoring runs, add your details, and start the first search.
2. [Today](guide/today.md): review new matches and work due now.
3. [Pipeline](guide/pipeline.md): search, filter, and decide which roles or leads deserve time.
4. [Role](guide/role.md): check the score, posting, organization, documents, and history.
5. [Applications and Leads](guide/applications.md): track stages, next actions, and follow-ups.

## Prepare

6. [Profile](guide/profile.md): tell Kestrel who you are and what work you want.
7. [Documents](guide/documents.md): keep the source text for your resume or capability statement.
8. [Tailor](guide/tailor.md): choose from your own saved wordings for one role.
9. [Interview and meeting prep](guide/prep.md): build a pack and practice.
10. [Story bank](guide/stories.md): save examples from your own work.
11. [Mock interview](guide/mock.md): rehearse questions in private.
12. [Offers and engagement terms](guide/offers.md): record, compare, and prepare a response.
13. [Company](guide/company.md): review sourced facts and public contacts.

## Run and improve the search

14. [Sources](guide/sources.md): choose public boards, searches, and signals.
15. [Runs](guide/runs.md): see what finished and what needs attention.
16. [Needs another try](guide/needs-another-try.md): recover roles whose scoring answer failed.
17. [Calibrate](guide/calibrate.md): record your judgment and review learning progress.
18. [Analytics](guide/analytics.md): read counts, charts, and local exports.
19. [Models](guide/models.md): see what reads your data and change a model.
20. [Model check](guide/model-check.md): compare models with your own ratings.

## App and reference

21. [Settings](guide/settings.md): control models, rules, privacy, data, and appearance.
22. [Help](guide/help.md): find built-in answers and replay the tour.
23. [About](guide/about.md): check the version, license, credits, and support details.

- [Settings reference](guide/settings-reference.md): every setting, its effect, and its default.
- [Privacy and online models](guide/privacy-and-online-models.md): local and online choices, ask-first dialogs, and confidential mode.
- [Troubleshooting](guide/troubleshooting.md): recover from messages shown by the app and its checks.
- [Keyboard and accessibility](guide/keyboard-and-accessibility.md): shortcuts, contrast, motion, and narrow windows.
