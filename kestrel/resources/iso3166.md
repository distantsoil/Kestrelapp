ISO 3166-1 country table

Manually transcribed on 2026-10-28 from the 249 assigned entries in:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ISO_3166_country_codes

Columns are alpha-2, alpha-3, three-digit numeric code (including leading zeros),
English short name, and pipe-separated common alternative names. Parenthetical
articles are also accepted as aliases. Aliases supplement the ISO entries and do
not assign new country codes. The table is shipped with Kestrel and never fetched
at runtime. Georgia retains its existing US state interpretation when spelled out.
