Kestrel is a free job search app for any job seeker, and you are not the product. It runs on your own computer (Windows, macOS or Linux), finds openings, scores them for fit, tailors your own resume text to each role, tracks every application to its outcome, and learns from the decisions you record. An optional second track covers consulting and contract work; it is off by default.

Your search stays on this computer. The AI runs here by default; you can choose an online model with your own key (OpenRouter, OpenModel, Anthropic, OpenAI or any OpenAI-compatible service), and Kestrel asks before anything goes online and shows exactly what would be sent. It never applies, emails or messages anyone for you; it drafts, recommends, and hands over.

Built with Claude, Qwen and NVIDIA Nemotron. The source is public under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 (see LICENSE.md).
