What happened

Kestrel asks an AI to read each role. Now and then the answer comes back in a shape Kestrel can’t read, takes too long, or an online service turns the request down. Kestrel always tries once more by itself first.

The three steps

Quick sort is a fast first look that keeps likely roles. Fit score rates each kept role against your profile. Deep read is the long look you ask for on one role.

What to do

Press Try again. Most roles go through the second time. If the same role keeps failing, try a different model.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('rt-safe-fly', 'Nothing is lost', help_href='/help') %}

Where the roles are

Every role here is still in Pipeline. It just has no score yet. Closing Kestrel or restarting your computer changes nothing, the list is saved as it goes.

When a role leaves this list

It leaves when it gets a score, or when you press Remove. Remove only takes it off this list. The role stays in Pipeline, marked as not scored, and Undo brings it back here.

Score it anyway

Skips your rules, such as place and pay limits, so a role you would normally set aside may be scored. Use it for a role you already want to look at.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('rt-model-fly', 'Trying a different model', help_href='/help') %}

Why it helps

Each model reads in its own way. A role one model can’t answer is often easy for another.

On your computer

Free. A smaller model is quicker but less careful. Kestrel says so before you pick.

Online

Uses your own credit and sends the role posting and a short summary of your profile to the service. Kestrel shows exactly what, and the cost, and waits for your yes. Your name and contact details are never sent.

If the key was refused

Open Models and check the key for that service. Until then, pick a model on your computer.
