{# Where the Record an offer sheet lands. #}
{% call ui.flyout('of-how-fly', 'How offers are compared', help_href='/help') %}

What is counted

First-year total is base, target bonus, signing pay once, and a quarter of your equity estimate. Four-year value assumes no raises, because nobody knows them yet.

How to use the weights

  • Move a slider to Ignore for anything that does not matter to you.
  • Rate the judgment rows yourself. Kestrel shows the fact beside the stars.
  • Read the Ahead on lines, then decide.

What it never does

It never picks a winner. This is your decision.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('of-walk-fly', 'Your walk-away is yours', help_href='/help') %}

Why it comes first

A draft without a floor can talk you into less than you would take. Setting the number first keeps the draft honest.

How to choose it

  • Start from your floor on the pay band.
  • Add what a move would cost you.
  • Round up, not down.
