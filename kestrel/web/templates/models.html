One model per step

Every role goes through up to five steps. Each step has a model, on this computer or online. The table shows which, and how well it matches your own ratings.

Changing one

Press Change on a row. Kestrel lists models that fit this computer first. A model that is not downloaded starts downloading when you pick it.

Is another model better for you?

Press Check my models. Kestrel tests the alternatives against an answer key, your own ratings once you use them, and tells you plainly whether to switch.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('mo-steps-fly', 'What each step does', help_href='/help') %}

Rules

Your own filters for title, place and pay. No AI.

Quick screen

Reads the start of each posting and sets aside clear misses. Uncertain roles are kept, never dropped.

Rating

Reads the whole posting and scores how well it fits you.

Resume picking

Chooses among your own resume lines. It never writes new ones.

Deeper read

Only when you ask, on one role. It checks the role and the company.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('mo-where-fly', 'On this computer or online', help_href='/help') %}

On this computer

Private and free. Nothing leaves your computer.

Online

Faster and paid per use, with your own key. The role text and the profile lines a step needs go to the provider, and Kestrel asks first. The model that finds similar roles always stays on this computer.

Both

Everything runs here except Deeper read, which runs online when you ask.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('mo-checks-fly', 'Checks and fixes', help_href='/help') %}

Room to read

How much text a model takes in at once, in pages of about 500 words. Too little and long postings are cut short without any warning.

The hidden-word test

Kestrel hides a word near the end of a long posting and asks the model to find it. That proves the model really read to the end.

Fix it

Rebuilds Kestrel's models with the right room to read, then tests again.
