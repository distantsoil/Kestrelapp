Calibrate

{# The intro panel (mockup section 9): what calibrating does, how many ratings, how long. #}

Rate scored roles the way you would judge them, so scoring learns your taste. It does not review your resume; the resume assessment lives on Profile > Documents.

  • What it does. Each rating is a label. At 15 Kestrel starts showing the model your own examples; with more, a ranker trained on your ratings can take over the order.
  • How many. 15 to start. The roles below are the ones Kestrel is least sure about, so each rating teaches it the most.
  • How long. About five minutes for 15: read the posting on the left, the evaluation on the right, then agree or pick your own fit word.
