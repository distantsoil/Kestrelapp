{# The toast containers and the scan signal (D-UI-36), included once from base.html. The status rail response (every 10 s) swaps #scan-signal out of band; the script below turns a new scan event into "Search done: N new. Still scoring M." (never "finished" while scoring continues, D-UI-63) and refreshes the Scan button when the scan state flips. The scan toast (#kestrel-toast) clears itself after 9 seconds, paused while the pointer or focus is on it, and goes sooner when it is dismissed (x, Escape while it has focus) or clicked; clicking opens the Pipeline on its New today chip (/opportunities?new_today=1), which is also where to find the result afterward. A scan with errors keeps the warning tone. Other callers of window.kestrelToast(text) go to the shared toast region (KUI.toast), where the scan toast also sits, so they never replace the scan toast or start a timer on it. Seen and dismissed state live in localStorage, so one tab's toast is the only one, and dismissing it in one tab clears it in the others. Styled by id and data attributes only, with the theme tokens, so both schemes and both themes follow. The scan button's pulse lives here too: this partial is on every page. It is part of the shell, outside the page's content root: it is not mounted or unmounted with a page (D-UI-210), and a toast, with its Undo, outlives the page that raised it. #}
