How the consulting track finds work (it is not a resume search)

Posted fractional and interim roles are a small market, so this track mostly works the other way around: it watches for trigger events, public facts that mean a company may soon need outside help in your field, and surfaces the company before a posting exists. An acquisition closing, a PE investment, a departed senior leader, a new compliance obligation. Those come from SEC filings and the news sources you turn on under Sources, plus any lead you enter by hand, which in consulting is the channel that converts best.

What it scores against is this tab: your service lines and target sectors and sizes decide whether a trigger is relevant, the rate floor and minimum engagement reject what is not worth pursuing. The document it tailors is a capability statement, imported on the Documents tab with kind "capability statement", not the resume. A pursuit here is an outreach thread (research, outreach, conversation, scoping, proposal), not an application.

Posted fractional roles are still caught: "Fractional and interim roles" under Sources > Your searches is an ordinary job search on this track, built from your titles and the service lines below.
