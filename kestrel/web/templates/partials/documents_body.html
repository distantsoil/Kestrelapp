What this means (click to expand)

Your resume is stored as building blocks so each application can use the right ones.

Role
One job, with its company, title and dates. "Edit role details" fixes anything the import misread. "Add a role" at the end of the list adds one.
Bullet
One achievement from that role, named by its first words. "Add a bullet" under a role's bullets adds one in your own words.
Your wording
Your own text, copied exactly from your resume. Edit it here and it saves on its own.
Alternate wording
A rewording of the same fact for a different audience. "Suggest wordings" asks the model for up to two; nothing is added until you click Add.
Themes
What the bullet shows (leadership, cost, security). They help the model pick the right wording for a posting.
Optional
Can be left out of a shorter resume.
Optional sections
Education, certifications, licenses and projects, typed in your own words. A section with no entries does not appear on the resume.
Accent color
The color of headings and rules. Pick a preset or type a six-digit hex color; it is saved for this document.

What to do now: read each role, fix any detail the import flagged, then check the preview. Preview as PDF makes the file. "Assess this resume" asks the model how it reads for your target titles; it only advises and never changes your text.
