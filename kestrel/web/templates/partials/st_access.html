Let other devices open Kestrel?

One shared key for the whole app, over plain HTTP. Anyone with the key can browse and work: read every role, document and your profile, score, track applications and keep notes. They cannot restore a backup, wipe data, download your data or change keys and where it goes. Only this computer can.

Use it only on a home network or a VPN you control. Never on shared, public or office Wi-Fi, and never through a port forward.
