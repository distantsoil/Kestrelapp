{% elif kind == 'feeds' %}

A feed (RSS or Atom) is a list of new items that a site publishes for apps to read. Company newsrooms often offer one. Each new item is read for signs a company will need help.

  1. Look for “RSS” or a feed icon on the site.
  2. Copy that link.
  3. Paste it here and check it.
