Public pages and filings only

Kestrel reads a company's own website, careers page and news page, and public filings such as annual reports. It never signs in anywhere and never uses private data.

How to tell what to trust

Every fact shows where it came from and when Kestrel saw it. Press the source to read the original. If a fact looks wrong, trust the source, not Kestrel.

Why some facts are missing

Small and private companies publish less. A blank is not a warning sign, it only means nothing public was found.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('co-sum-fly', 'The summary', help_href='/help') %}

Where it comes from

The AI writes it from the public sources listed in What Kestrel knows. The small numbers point to the source for each sentence.

When there is not enough

With fewer than two public sources Kestrel writes nothing and does not ask a model to guess.

Refresh

Refresh writes it again. On a model on this computer it just runs. On an online model Kestrel shows exactly what is sent and waits for your yes.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('co-hide-fly', 'Hiding a company', help_href='/help') %}

What Hide does

Roles from this company stop showing in Pipeline and Today. Kestrel still keeps what it already found, so you can change your mind.

How to undo

Press Undo right after, or Show this company here. You can also manage the whole list in Settings under Hidden companies.

Hide or just pass

Hide when you never want to see them again. To pass on one role, use Not interested on that role instead.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.flyout('co-reach-fly', 'Reaching people', help_href='/help') %}

What you will see

Names, titles and business contact details that the company has made public, such as a team page. Each one shows where it was found.

People you know here

Only if you imported your LinkedIn connections file, which you download from LinkedIn yourself. Kestrel reads it on your own computer, never logs in to LinkedIn, and lists only people who work here now or used to, based on your earlier import.

What to do with it

You decide whether to write to someone, and you write it yourself. A draft is text you copy and send from your own email.
