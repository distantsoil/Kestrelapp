{# Reference article: Kestrel from another device. /help#lan opens it in the reader. #}

Optional, and off by default. Kestrel is a web page served by the desktop app, so it can also be opened from a phone, tablet or laptop on the same home network: read Today, review the pipeline, check an application or your interview prep away from the desk. Nothing moves: the models, the database and the documents stay on this machine, which has to be running (in the window or the tray).

Turning it on is a switch on Settings, Privacy and access. You generate one shared secret, tick that you read what it does and does not protect, and turn the switch on; it takes effect after you quit and reopen Kestrel. Then you share the secret with each device. If you only use Kestrel at this computer, leave it off.
