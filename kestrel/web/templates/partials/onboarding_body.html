Kestrel finds and ranks work for you, and leaves every decision to you. It searches public job boards and filings, reads each posting with an AI model, drops the ones that clearly do not fit, scores the rest against what you want, and tailors your resume for the ones worth pursuing, using only your own words.

What it never does. Kestrel never applies, never sends an email or message, and never fills in a form for you. It drafts and recommends; you decide and you send.

Your privacy. Everything stays on this computer by default. If you choose a hosted AI provider in the next step, only the text you agree to is sent to that provider, under your own key, and you read exactly what that is before you connect.

How long this takes. About ten minutes of your time. If you run the models on this computer, the first download can take longer; you can keep going while it runs.

Kestrel never sees your bill and never sends the key anywhere else.
