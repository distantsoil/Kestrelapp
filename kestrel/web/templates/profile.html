What each tab is for

About you heads your documents. Documents holds your resume in your own words. Employment{% if consulting_enabled() %}, Consulting{% endif %} and Location are what the filters and the scoring read: a role outside them is set aside before any model sees it.

Saving

The About you, Employment, Consulting and Location tabs show a Save bar when you change something. Save or discard before you switch tabs. After saving, Undo brings back the last version. Documents save as you type.

Check your filters

After a change, Settings > Filters shows what the new terms would set aside.
