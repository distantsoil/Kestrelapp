Kestrel Capture

This extension talks only to your local Kestrel. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Leave the port blank and it finds Kestrel by itself. Set one only to pin a specific instance; the running port is on Kestrel's About page (it is the port in runtime.json).

Kestrel accepts a capture or a badge lookup from an extension only when it carries this secret, so that any other extension with a localhost permission cannot drive your Kestrel. Generate it in Kestrel under Settings > LAN and paste it here. It is sent as the X-Kestrel-Key header on real requests, never on a discovery probe.
