    What this extension can see and do

    • On the job boards it supports, it checks the links already on the page against Kestrel and shows a small badge next to postings Kestrel knows.
    • When you click Capture or Read, it reads the title and text of the page you have open and sends them to Kestrel on this computer.
    • When you open its popup, it sends the address of the tab you are on to Kestrel on this computer, to see whether Kestrel knows that posting.
    • It shows what Kestrel already knows about a role: its score, why it fits, and your documents for it.

    What it never does

    • It never applies, fills in a form, clicks or submits anything for you.
    • It never opens, reloads or edits a page, and never gets past a login or a check page. A badge is its own small element next to a link; the page's text and links stay as they were.
    • It never sends anything to any server but Kestrel on this computer. Nothing is collected, sold or shared.
